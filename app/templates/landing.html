Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Yes, absolutely. All data is isolated per user and hosted on secure cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, or your own environment). We implement enterprise-grade security with SOC2 compliance and zero data sharing with public LLMs.

Can I use my preferred LLM?

Absolutely. Choose from ChatGPT, Claude, LLaMA2, or other leading models to power your queries. You can even switch between models based on different use cases or team preferences.

What happens after the free trial?

You can select a paid plan based on your usage and features. We offer transparent pricing with no surprises or hidden fees. You can also continue with our free tier for basic usage.

How fast is the deployment process?

From POC to production in under 3 weeks. Our team works with you to ensure smooth integration with your existing infrastructure and workflows. Most clients see value within the first week.

What data sources can I connect?

We support 20+ data sources including Snowflake, AWS RDS, Azure SQL, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, CSVs, Excel files, and many more. If you have a custom data source, our team can help build a connector.
